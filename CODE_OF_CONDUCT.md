# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Flutter Kickstart to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good ways to take part:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept helpful feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

Not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and the
docs site), and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer,
[@alamin-karno](https://github.com/alamin-karno), privately through GitHub.
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
